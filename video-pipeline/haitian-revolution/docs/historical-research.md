# Historical Research Notes (Haitian Revolution)

This project chronology is built from mainstream historical references and archives before scene generation.

## Core timeline checkpoints used

1. **Bois Caïman ceremony** linked to planning of coordinated revolt in **August 1791**.
2. **Mass slave uprising begins** in northern Saint-Domingue in **late August 1791**.
3. **French abolition in colonies (1794)** shifts alliances; Toussaint Louverture joins French Republican side.
4. Multi-front war years include conflicts against **Spanish and British** forces.
5. **Toussaint Louverture captured in June 1802** and deported to France; dies at Fort de Joux in 1803.
6. Renewed resistance led by **Dessalines and Christophe** against Napoleon's expedition.
7. **Battle of Vertières on 18 November 1803** as decisive final major battle.
8. **Independence declared on 1 January 1804** in Gonaïves by Dessalines.

## Accuracy guardrails applied in scene design

- No modern weapons, uniforms, vehicles, or architecture.
- No fantasy magic visuals or mythical effects.
- Battlefield direction favors formation movement, artillery pressure, fort assaults, and command decisions.
- Character consistency maintained for Toussaint Louverture, Jean-Jacques Dessalines, and Henri Christophe.
